## Text sources not in the look brief's reference list

- Snapmaker U1 specs (270 × 270 × 270 mm, four toolheads, 0.4 mm nozzles, Snapmaker Orca): [snapmaker.com specs](https://www.snapmaker.com/snapmaker-u1/specs), reviews at [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/3d-printing/snapmaker-u1-review) and [VoxelMatters](https://www.voxelmatters.com/voxelmatters-review-of-the-snapmaker-u1-simply-the-best-4-color-3d-printer/).
- PLA softening and creep: heat deflection quoted between 50 and 60 °C depending on source; loaded parts creep below that. [JLC3DP](https://jlc3dp.com/blog/pla-temperature-resistance), [Makers101](https://makers101.com/heat-resistant-3d-printing-filament/).
- PLA friction and wear: one lab study gives a friction coefficient near 0.54 for PLA against polyamide; guidance favours PTFE or silicone grease. No cycle-life data found. [Polymaker wiki](https://wiki.polymaker.com/the-basics/fun-3d-printing-facts/wear-resistant-3d-printing-materials), [igus](https://toolbox.igus.com/?p=3479).
- Commercial wave-machine plan quoting 8 to 32 slats, half to three waves, 10 to 40 mm height: [atomm.com](https://www.atomm.com/creativetools/community/kinetic-wave-machine).
- Nikiszowiec background: [National Geographic Polska](https://www.national-geographic.pl/traveler/kierunki/nikiszowiec-perla-katowickiej-architektury-industrialnej/), [In Your Pocket](https://www.inyourpocket.com/poland/katowice/articles/Nikiszowiec-and-Giszowiec).
- Tilt threshold of 25 mm/m: search summary of a [geoforum.pl article](https://geoforum.pl/wydanie/1259/artykul/2573/archiwum-geodety/wydanie/254.pdf), not read directly.
