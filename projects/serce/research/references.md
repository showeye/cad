# Serce na węgiel: annotated reference register

Research checked on 2026-11-12. These are photographs of physical objects, museum documentation
and first-party manufacturing information. Design conclusions are our interpretation of the
references, not claims made by their authors. The concept renders in `docs/img/` are our own CAD
studies and are not evidence of print quality.

## Coal and the carving tradition

1. [Anthracite, Sandatlas](https://sandatlas.org/anthracite/): use broad reflective fracture faces
   interrupted by smaller breaks; the sample photographs explain why uniformly matte black loses
   the coal character. Local photographs: `refs/coal_sandatlas_anthracite_8cm.jpg` and
   `refs/coal_sandatlas_anthracite_ukraine.jpg`.
2. [Ibbenbüren anthracite, Educerva](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ibbenbueren_Anthracite.JPG):
   preserve uneven face sizes and sharp changes of direction rather than a regular triangle grid.
   Local: `refs/coal_anthracite_ibbenbueren.jpg`; CC BY-SA 3.0.
3. [Coalmont bituminous specimen, Adam Kelliher / Rubble Rock and Gem](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bituminous_coal_specimen,_Coalmont,_British_Columbia,_Canada.jpg):
   take the layered fracture rhythm as a secondary cue; avoid turning the whole heart into equal
   horizontal stripes. Local: `refs/coal_bituminous_coalmont.jpg`; CC BY-SA 4.0.
4. [Saint Barbara carved in coal, Abraham](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20221213_Saint_Barbara_in_coal.jpg):
   use the local tradition of a figurative object emerging from dark material; keep our silhouette
   simpler. Local: `refs/carving_st_barbara_coal.jpg`; CC BY-SA 4.0 per the saved metadata.
   The source page could not be reopened in this session; attribution remains to be rechecked.
5. [“Rzeźbiarze w węglu”, Muzeum Górnictwa Węglowego](https://muzeumgornictwa.pl/gdy-wegiel-staje-sie-sztuka-zobacz-film-rzezbiarze-w-weglu/):
   the museum's documentary establishes an active regional carving tradition and everyday-life
   subjects; borrow that connection, with an affectionate domestic scene.

## Heart silhouette

6. [Anatomical heart, Deutsches Hygiene-Museum, photograph by Dguendel](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dresden,_Deutsches_Hygiene-Museum,_anatomisches_Modell,_Herz.jpg):
   study the compact, irregular mass, but omit vessels and medical detail for immediate recognition.
   Local: `refs/heart_anatomical_model_dresden.jpg`; CC BY 4.0.
7. [Heart-shaped rings, JewelsExpert](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Heart_Shape_Rings.jpg):
   retain the cleft and tapered lower silhouette; gemstone symmetry is the comparison concept,
   rather than the recommended coal treatment. Local: `refs/heart_gem_rings.jpg`; CC BY-SA 4.0.
8. [Gingerbread hearts](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gingerbread_hearts.jpg):
   a useful warning about souvenir sweetness; omit borders, bows and exterior lettering.
   Local: `refs/heart_gingerbread.jpg`. Saved metadata lacks the author's name; recheck before reuse.

## Silesian home

9. [“U nos w doma na Nikiszu”, Muzeum Historii Katowic](https://mhk.katowice.pl/wystawy-stale/u-nos-w-doma-na-nikiszu-we-wnetrzu-nikiszowieckiego-mieszkania/):
   use a kitchen as the focal room, with domestic objects from the museum's 1930s–1960s context;
   this is a condensed scene, not a reconstruction of the exhibition's floor plan.
   Saved gallery photographs: `refs/home_mhk_nikisz_*.jpg`; individual image credits need review.
10. [Traditional kitchen, Anna Lerch-Wójcik / Bonclok](https://bonclok.pl/slaskie-tradycje/codziennosc/259-wyposazenie-tradycyjnej-kuchni.html):
    combine white joinery, a stove, a table and window plants; those large shapes should read before
    small decoration. Local: `refs/home_bonclok_kuchnia_*.jpg`.
11. [Stove photographs, Bonclok](https://bonclok.pl/slaskie-tradycje/codziennosc/256-piec-nie-tylko-do-ogrzewania.html):
    use a tiled body, iron cooking surface, pot and coal scuttle to make the “runs on coal” joke
    visible. Local: `refs/home_bonclok_piec_1.jpg`, `refs/home_bonclok_piec_2.jpg`.
12. [Byfyj and household vessels, Bonclok](https://bonclok.pl/slaskie-tradycje/codziennosc/429-w-antryju-na-byfyju-stoji-szolka-tyju.html):
    reduce the dresser to a white lower cupboard and framed upper cabinet.
    Local: `refs/home_bonclok_byfyj.jpg`.
13. [Embroidered kitchen hangings, Bonclok](https://bonclok.pl/slaskie-tradycje/codziennosc/428-haftowane-makatki-z-madrosciami-domowymi.html):
    use a pale rectangle with a coarse relief border; omit miniature writing.
    Local: `refs/home_bonclok_makatka_swieza_woda.jpg`.

## The reveal

14. [Hen Egg, Fabergé Museum catalogue](https://fabergemuseum.ru/faberge_expo_en.pdf):
    take the quiet exterior and strong material/colour surprise, without copying the egg's form or
    construction. Local photograph: `refs/reveal_faberge_first_hen.jpg` (credits in
    `commons_metadata.json`). The collection webpage redirected to ticketing during verification;
    the museum catalogue remains the institutional reference.
15. [Closed Japanese puzzle box, Eugene Alvin Villar](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Japanese_puzzle_box_(yosegi)_-_closed.jpg):
    repeat surface cues so a real joint does not announce itself as a door outline.
    Local: `refs/reveal_puzzle_box_closed.jpg`; CC BY-SA 4.0.
16. [The same box open, Eugene Alvin Villar](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Japanese_puzzle_box_(yosegi)_-_opened.jpg):
    retain the satisfaction of an unexpected cavity while making ours operable with one simple
    turn. Local: `refs/reveal_puzzle_box_opened.jpg`; CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Printed surfaces and colour

17. [Physical low-poly print, Creative Tools](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Low_Poly_Stanford_Bunny.jpg):
    use broad facets that survive layer texture. Local: `refs/print_low_poly_bunny.jpg`; CC BY 2.0.
    The source does not establish PLA or U1 settings, so this supports shape treatment only.
18. [Four-colour PLA first print, Coast2Hills Creations / Snapmaker Space](https://space.snapmaker.com/en/model/41815-snappy-u1-4-color-print-in-place-first-print):
    inspect the maker's physical print and coarse colour boundaries; the indexed author description
    identifies four-colour PLA, but the dynamic page was only partly readable. This is qualitative
    evidence, not a benchmark for our tolerances or print time.
19. [Black PLA caliper, its maker's photographs and material description](https://www.reddit.com/r/3Dprinting/comments/ha7npe):
    inspect how black PLA highlights layers and edges on a real small object. The author identifies
    Galaxy Black PLA; that glitter-filled finish is not the proposed standard black filament.

## Existing souvenirs

20. [Heart on a stand, Artis](https://www.artis-pamiatkigornicze.pl/oferta/serca-na-grob):
    the static coal-heart silhouette already exists; our distinction is the controlled domestic
    reveal. The page describes plaster hearts coated with coal/dolomite and varnish, so do not
    describe this product as carved solid coal. Local: `refs/market_artis_coal_heart_on_stand.webp`.
    Search-index text was readable; directly fetching this page failed.
21. [Coal on a stand, Artis](https://www.artis-pamiatkigornicze.pl/oferta/wegiel-na-podstawce):
    make the base subordinate to the sculpture and keep plaques off the front.
    Local: `refs/market_artis_coal_on_stand.webp`.
22. [Coal keyrings and magnets, Artis](https://www.artis-pamiatkigornicze.pl/oferta/breloczki-i-magnesy):
    distinguish the collector piece through movement, precision and serviceability rather than
    adding more mining symbols. This is a limited market sample, not an exhaustive novelty search.

## Engineering sources

- [Snapmaker U1, manufacturer](https://www.snapmaker.com/en-US/snapmaker-u1): first-party machine
  reference for four toolheads and the 270 × 270 × 270 mm envelope. Advertised speed is not a
  measured production time for this design.
- [Prusament PLA technical datasheet](https://prusament.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/PLA_Prusament_TDS_2021_10_EN.pdf):
  lists 55 °C heat-deflection temperature at the stated ISO 75 test loads. This is a material test,
  not an allowable continuous-use temperature for this assembly.

## Image provenance and remaining gaps

`commons_metadata.json` preserves the previously collected Commons author and licence records.
Bonclok identifies the photographic author as Anna Lerch-Wójcik; no open reuse licence has been
established for those images, the MHK photographs or the Artis product photographs. Keep them as
internal research references; marketing artwork should use our own object and renders. No source
photograph has been added to a new public-facing marketing asset in this continuation.

The generic low-poly photograph does not verify black PLA performance. The black-PLA maker
photograph is not a U1 print. The U1 example does not prove this scene's tiny details. Actual
four-colour U1 coupons are needed before approving production surfaces or minimum feature sizes.
